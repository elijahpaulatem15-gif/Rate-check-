Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Rate Check. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information is handled when you use our currency converter and cryptocurrency rate checker.

2. Information We Store

Rate Check does not require users to create an account or provide their names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

The app stores certain preferences and information locally on your device, including:

  • Currency conversion amounts.
  • Your selected starting currency.
  • Your selected destination currency.
  • Your selected cryptocurrency.
  • Your recent currency conversion history.

This information is stored using your browser's local storage. It is used to remember your preferences and display your recent conversions.

3. How We Use Information

Locally stored information helps Rate Check remember your settings and provide its currency conversion and history features.

Currency and cryptocurrency selections are used to request the relevant rates from external data providers.

4. Third-Party Services

Rate Check uses third-party services to retrieve financial information:

  • Frankfurter API — provides currency information and exchange rates.
  • CoinGecko — provides cryptocurrency prices and 24-hour price changes.

When the app requests information from these services, technical information such as your IP address, request details, and connection information may be processed by the service providers.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of information is outside Rate Check's direct control.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Rate Check uses browser local storage to save preferences, conversion amounts, and recent conversion history.

The app does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party advertising trackers in its current implementation.

The app also uses a service worker to cache website files so that previously downloaded content may remain available when your device is offline.

6. Managing Your Information

You can clear your recent conversion history using the Clear History button inside Rate Check.

You can remove saved preferences and other locally stored information by clearing the site's data through your browser settings.

Clearing site data may also remove cached app files and require the app to download them again.

7. Children's Privacy

Rate Check is a general-purpose currency and cryptocurrency information tool. It is not specifically designed for children, and it does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect the app and its information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when Rate Check's features, services, or information practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
